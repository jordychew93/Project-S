How health works

  • Money = body size. More money, bigger blob. Everyone starts at $100,000.
  • Health = distance from its best. 100 at a new high. It loses 5 points for every 1% the book falls from its own best balance.
  • Knocked out (x x eyes, tipped over) at a 20% fall from its best, or if its money halves. That is the arena's own 20% drop rule. Out means out for the rest of the run.
